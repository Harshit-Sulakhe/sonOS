QEMU powers on
    ↓
BIOS runs (emulated by QEMU)
    ↓
GRUB bootloader loads from ISO
    ↓
GRUB reads grub.cfg → finds myos.bin
    ↓
GRUB loads myos.bin into memory at 0x100000
    ↓
GRUB switches CPU to 32-bit protected mode
    ↓
GRUB jumps to _start in boot.asm
    ↓
boot.asm sets up the stack
    ↓
boot.asm calls kernel_main() in C
    ↓
kernel_main() writes "Hello from MyOS!" to 0xB8000
    ↓
Text appears on screen
